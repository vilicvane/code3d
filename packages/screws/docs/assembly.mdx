## Screw and clearance tool

`ISO4762.screw('M6', 18)` creates a socket cap screw.
`ISO4762.clearanceHole('M6', 10)` creates its clearance and counterbore tool.
The hole is a model used with `cut(stock, [hole])` and provides named mounting
references.

<ModelExample id="screw-box" />

The lid exposes four seats from the holes' `counterboreBottom` references.
Each screw's `headBottom` meets one seat, and the lid's `mountingFace` meets
the box's `lidSeat`. Set `gap` to 0 to close the box or lift the lid and screws
together to inspect the holes. The box models blind tapping pilots; internal
thread geometry is omitted.

See [clearance holes and mounting](clearance-holes.md) for recess options and references,
and [standards and specifications](standards.md) to choose a fastener.
